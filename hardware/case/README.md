# Gree bridge rev B case

Printed case for the Gree bridge rev B board (ESP32-S3, 75 x 54 mm), which sits inside a Gree GWH12KF indoor unit and runs from the unit's 12 V logic supply through J1. The board is frozen, so the case is built around it as it is. Every position comes from `gree_bridge.kicad_pcb`: outline, the 2 milled BME280 slots, H1-H3, the fab outline of each part, and the pads.

**Case: 80.0 x 69.55 x 25.3 mm** (X x Y x height); 114 mm across the two ears. The back has a 32 x 10.5 mm bump that keeps 5 mm of air around the antenna. Three printed parts in PETG, no supports. Closed with 3 x M3 x 20 screws. **Fit check: 308 rows, all pass** (`out/fit_check.txt`).

Design status: designed and checked in CAD (October 2026), not printed yet.

Needed to rebuild: openscad (2021.01 or newer), python3 with `pcbnew` (KiCad 7), cadquery, trimesh, manifold3d, shapely, vtk and matplotlib.

Frame: X = kicad_x - 97.5, Y = 156.5 - kicad_y, Z = 0 on the mounting face. Seen from above with the lid on, the case reads like KiCad's top view: antenna at the back (+Y), J1 on the left, BME280 at the front right.

## How it goes together

- **Base** (tray, 2 mm walls, 2 mm floor, flat back). The board sits on **3 bosses at H1-H3**, 5 mm above the floor. The bosses are 6.4 mm in diameter, inside the 6.6 mm screw-head keep-out rings. Two **rest ribs** carry the plug-in forces without touching any solder joint: one next to the J1 pins, one next to J5. The board has 0.5 mm of clearance to the wall on every side.
- **Lid** (2 mm plate with a 3 mm locating lip, 0.3 mm fit). Three columns press the board onto the bosses. The lid rides on those columns, so the board is always clamped. The rim floats 0.2 mm over the wall top; with a +10 % thick PCB this gap is still 0.04 mm.
- **Closing: 3 x M3 x 20 pan head** (ISO 7045 / ISO 14583) go in from the lid top. Each one passes through a lid column and a board hole and threads into a base boss. They are **self-tapping into 2.5 mm pilots** (5.7 mm of thread engagement). The heads sit 1.6 mm below the top surface, and the screw tips stop 1.3 mm above the mounting face, so the back stays closed. For brass inserts instead, set `use_inserts=True` (M3 inserts in a 4.0 mm hole, 5.5 mm deep). The nearest boss is 32 mm from the antenna.
- **J1 cable**: plug the XHP-5 in from above and bend the 5 wires to the left. They leave through a **13 x 4.0 mm window** in the left wall, which the lid closes from above. Under the window, outside the wall, a **zip-tie block** has a 3.6 x 1.8 mm tunnel. Put a small tie (2.5 mm, up to 3.6 mm) through the tunnel and around the cable for strain relief.
- **J5 programming**: a 4.6 x 17.8 mm opening sits over J5, closed by the **snap cap** (third part, flush with the top). Pull the cap out with a fingernail in the notch, then plug the USB-UART Dupont 1x6. Pin 1 (GND) is at the "1" mark, at the antenna end. The housing pushes down onto the header with 1 mm of side clearance.
- **BOOT**: a printed **flexure key** in the lid, marked with a dot. It is a 1 mm tongue, 5 x 11.8 mm, with a 3 mm stem that stops 0.3 mm over the switch. A 0.75 mm stroke puts 1.6 % strain on the PETG. Press it 6 times for the Wi-Fi setup hotspot.
- **RESET**: a **2.2 mm pin hole** with a guide tube that ends 1 mm over the switch. Use a paper clip. It is a pin hole so the board can't be reset by accident.
- **LEDs**: 2.4 mm light holes, each with a light tube down to 2.5 mm above the LED: PWR (D4, red) and LINK (D3, green, Tasmota LedLink). The tubes stop light leaking from one LED to the other. For a diffusing window instead of an open hole, set `led_skin=0.4` and print in natural or white PETG.
- **BME280 corner**: an L-shaped **baffle** hangs from the lid along the two milled slots. It sits 0.6 mm above the board, 0.65 mm from the breakout and 0.75 mm from J3. A matching rib on the floor runs under the slots. Vents: 5 slots in the lid over the breakout (80 mm²), plus slots in the right and front walls from the floor up (219 mm²).
- **General vents**: 6 lid slots over the buck regulator and LDO, 7 slots in the left wall and 5 in the antenna bump. Set `vents_lid_general` / `vents_lid_bme` to `False` if the mounting spot can get drips or dust from above.
- **J3 knock-out**: the front wall has a window like J1's, closed by a 0.6 mm skin. Cut it out with a flush cutter if a J3 cable is ever fitted. The lip is open there, and a fitted J3 plug still clears the lid by 4.9 mm.
- **Mounting**, any of these:
  - 2 ears, placed diagonally: left-front and right-back. Each has a **4.3 mm hole** for an M4 / #8 pan head (head 8 mm or smaller) and **two 2.0 x 5.2 mm zip-tie slots** for ties up to 4.8 mm.
  - The **flat back** for double-sided foam tape.

  The ear screws are 17.6 mm from the antenna.
- **Text**: "GREE BRIDGE" is raised letters set in a 0.8 mm pocket, with the letter tops flush with the lid top. Raised text that stands proud of the face can't print on the face that lies on the bed; this way it looks embossed and still prints without supports. PWR / LINK / RST / BOOT / PROG / 1 / I2C are debossed 0.6 mm. "GREE BRIDGE rev B" is debossed on the front wall.

### Assembly order

1. Trim the U5 header pins on the back. Untrimmed (4.4 mm) still clears the floor by 0.6 mm.
2. Drop the board into the base with the antenna toward the bump.
3. Plug J1, lay the wires flat in the left window, and zip-tie the cable to the block under the window.
4. Fit the lid and the 3 x M3 x 20 screws. Tighten until the lid stops, and no further: they are self-tapping in PETG.
5. Snap in the J5 cap and mount the case: ear screws, zip ties, or tape.

## Print

PETG, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 4 perimeters (the 2 mm walls come out solid), 20 % gyroid infill, **no supports**, no brim needed. The base has a 0.6 mm chamfer on its bottom edge and the lid a 0.8 mm chamfer on its top edge, to absorb elephant's foot.

| Part | Orientation | Size on the bed | Material |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | floor down, as exported | 114 x 69.6 x 23.1 mm | 23.5 cm³ solid, about 25 g |
| Lid | **top face down** (the label and key face the bed) | 80 x 69.6 x 16.7 mm | 11.5 cm³, about 13 g |
| J5 cap | flange down | 6 x 19.2 x 3.6 mm | under 0.3 g |

The widest unsupported bridge is 3.6 mm (top of the zip-tie tunnel) on the base and 3.2 mm (the BOOT dot) on the lid. The fit check measures these on the print-orientation STLs.

Hardware:
- 3 x M3 x 20 pan head screws, or the same plus 3 x M3 heat-set inserts
- 1 zip tie, 2.5 mm, for J1
- for mounting, either 2 x M4 / #8 screws, zip ties up to 4.8 mm, or foam tape

## Fit check (summary of `out/fit_check.txt`)

Distances are exact mesh-to-envelope minimum gaps (manifold3d), measured on the OpenSCAD meshes in the assembled position. Component envelopes are the KiCad F.Fab outlines with the heights listed under Assumptions.

| Item (height above board) | Gap to lid above | Smallest 3D gap to case |
|---|---|---|
| L1 inductor (4.5) | 10.20 | 5.45 |
| U4 ESP32 module (3.25) | 11.45 | 3.36 |
| SW1 RESET actuator (4.3) | 1.00 (guide tube) | 1.00 |
| SW2 BOOT actuator (4.3) | **0.30 (stem, by design)** | 0.30 |
| SW2 body (3.5) | 1.10 | 0.87 (H3 column) |
| J1 header (7.0) | 7.70 | 1.55 |
| **J1 with XHP-5 plugged (9.8)** | **4.90** | 1.55 |
| J1 wires, 1.9 mm OD worst case, r 2.5 bend (14.2) | 0.50 | 0.50 |
| J3 header / optional J3 plug (7.0 / 9.8) | 7.70 / 4.90 | 0.75 (baffle) |
| J5 pins (8.54) | open (opening) | 6.47 |
| Dupont 1x6 on J5, cap off | through the opening | 1.03 |
| U5 BME280 breakout (6.0) | 5.70 | 0.65 (baffle) |

- **Walls**: the board is 0.50 mm from the wall on all 4 sides. The lid lip is 0.30 mm from the base wall. The cap is 0.30 mm from its recess and 0.15 mm from the opening, with a 0.3 mm snap.
- **Antenna**: there is 5.00 mm of air from the antenna end to the nearest plastic (no plastic within 5 mm).
  - nearest M3 screw: 34.9 mm (5 mm required, 15 mm recommended for metal)
  - nearest boss or insert position: 32.0 mm
  - ear screws: 17.6 mm
  - nearest rest rib: 6.4 mm
- **Under the board** (5.0 mm gap): untrimmed U5 pins 0.6 mm, J1/J3 tails (3.4 mm) 1.5 mm, J5 tails 1.35 mm. The bosses and rest ribs stay 1.1 mm or more from every THT pad.
- **Mesh checks**: no base/lid/cap/board overlaps. Each part is one watertight body, and the mounting face is closed. All 308 rows pass.

## Assumptions

- **Heights above the board** (datasheet maxima):
  - ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 3.25; L1 VLS6045EX 4.5
  - PTS645SL43: 4.3 to the actuator, 3.5 body, 0.25 travel
  - XH header 7.0; XHP mated 9.8 (JST "mounting height")
  - J5: 2.54 body + 6 mm pins = 8.54; Dupont housing 14.5
  - SMA 2.6, SOT-223 1.8, SOIC 1.75, 1206 caps 1.8, LEDs 1.2
- **GY-BME280** sits flat on a 2.5 mm header spacer (11.6 x 15.3 mm, chip up). Its top, including the short pins and solder, is taken as 6.0 mm above the board, which allows a PCB up to 1.6 mm thick.
- **Below the board**: XH tails 3.4, J5 tails 1.7, U5 pins 4.4 if untrimmed, solder at the ESP32 centre hole 1.0.
- **J1 wires**: 5 wires at the crimp's maximum OD of 1.9 mm (AWG 22), inner bend radius 2.5 mm. With AWG 26-28 the wires clear the lid by about 1 mm or more.
- PCB thickness 1.6 mm ±10 %. Fab outlines are used as body envelopes (+0.05 mm for line width).
- Screws: ISO 7045 M3 pan head (head 5.6 x 2.4 mm). Ear screws M4 / #8 with a head of 8 mm or less.

## Before you print

The case was designed and checked in CAD only; it has **not been printed yet**. Things to check for your own unit:

1. **Where it goes in the unit**: for example on the side of the electrical box or on the chassis next to it. Keep the antenna bump at least 15 mm from sheet metal, ideally pointing toward the front grille. If the spot can get drips or dust from above (under the coil or the drain pan), turn the lid vents off (`vents_lid_general`, `vents_lid_bme`).
2. **Cable exit**: the J1 cable leaves through the left wall at lid height. A top exit would make the case about 3.5 mm lower but is not drawn.
3. **Lid fixing**: self-tapping M3 into PETG (default) or heat-set inserts (`use_inserts=True`).
4. **BOOT key feel**: print the lid once and try the key; `stem_gap` (0.3) and `boot_t` (1.0) are the knobs.
5. **LEDs**: open holes (default) or a thin diffuser skin (`led_skin=0.4`, natural or white PETG).
6. **Your board**: the BME280 breakout top must be 6.0 mm or less above the PCB, and the J1 wires 1.9 mm OD or less.

After changing a knob in `layout_gree.py`, run `./build.sh` again: it rebuilds the STL and STEP files, reruns the fit check (exits 1 if anything fails) and the renders.
