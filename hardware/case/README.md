# Gree bridge rev B case

Printed case for the Gree bridge rev B board (ESP32-S3, 75 x 54 mm), which sits inside a Gree GWH12KF indoor unit and runs from the unit's 12 V logic supply through J1. The board is frozen, so the case is built around it as it is. Every position comes from `gree_bridge.kicad_pcb`: outline, the 2 milled BME280 slots, H1-H3, the fab outline of each part, and the pads.

**Case: 80.0 x 69.55 x 25.3 mm** (X x Y x height); 114 mm across the two ears. The back has a 32 x 10.5 mm bump that keeps 5 mm of air around the antenna. Three printed parts in PETG, no supports.

**Rev 2:**
- The board is screwed to the base with 3 x M3 x 6 pan head screws.
- The lid **clicks on** with 3 snap latches and sits on the wall top. Its top is clean: no screw holes.

**Fit check: 349 rows, all pass** (`out/fit_check.txt`).

Design status: designed and checked in CAD (rev 2, 2026-10-18), not printed yet.

## How it goes together

- **Base** (tray, 2 mm walls, 2 mm floor, flat back).
  - The board sits on **3 bosses at H1-H3**, 5 mm above the floor. The bosses are 6.4 mm in diameter, inside the 6.6 mm keep-out rings.
  - It is held by **3 x M3 x 6 pan head screws** from above, self-tapping into 2.5 mm pilots: 4.4 mm of thread in the boss, tips 2.6 mm above the mounting face.
  - M3 x 6 is the longest standard length that fits: an M3 x 8 would come within 0.6 mm of the back.
  - Two rest ribs carry the plug-in forces, next to the J1 pins and next to J5. Board-to-wall clearance is 0.5 mm.
  - **Heat-set inserts** (`use_inserts=True`): M3 inserts up to 4 mm long in a 4.0 x 5.0 mm hole. The boss grows to 7.4 mm (1.7 mm wall); plastic beyond the copper keep-out ring does no harm. The M3 x 6 then threads the full 4 mm of the insert. The nearest boss is 32 mm from the antenna.
- **Lid** (2 mm plate with a 3 mm locating lip, 0.3 mm fit). It **sits on the wall top**. No lid feature touches the board: the closest is the BME baffle, 0.8 mm above it. The lid keeps at least 1.05 mm of air around each screw head.
- **Snap latches** (3x: back wall at kicad x 119.5, front wall at kicad x 119.5, right wall at kicad y 128):
  - **Arm**: a cantilever cut from the lip with 0.8 mm slits. 1.3 mm thick, 7 mm wide, 9.2 mm from the plate to the top of the hook. The arm bottom is 2.1 mm above the board.
  - **Hook**: stands 1.1 mm proud of the arm. A 30° lead-in ramp below; a 45° catch face above.
  - **Window**: 7.8 x 2.7 mm, straight through the wall. A 0.6 mm chamfer on the wall top leads the hook in.
  - **Strain**: the hook deflects 0.8 mm to pass the wall, 1.84 % peak strain.
  - **Preload**: the catch face bears on the window's top edge with 0.3 mm of preload (0.10 mm worst case), which pulls the lid down onto the walls so it doesn't rattle. Engagement is 0.5 mm (0.3 mm worst case).
  - **Forces** (estimated): about 7 N per latch to click on, about 12 N per latch to pull off.
  - **Clearance**: arms and hooks clear the board and every part by 1.98 mm at rest and 1.38 mm fully deflected (SW2's leads at the right latch).
- **To open**: put a small flat screwdriver (2 mm or less) into a latch window. Push the hook in about 0.5 mm; its tip sits 1.5 mm inside the wall's outer face. Lift that side a little, then do the next latch. A firm pull also releases them.
- **J1 cable**: plug the XHP-5 in from above and bend the 5 wires to the left. They leave through a **13 x 4.0 mm window** in the left wall, which the lid closes from above. Under the window, outside the wall, a **zip-tie block** has a 3.6 x 1.8 mm tunnel. Put a small tie through it and around the cable for strain relief.
- **J5 programming**: a 4.6 x 17.8 mm opening sits over J5, closed by the **snap cap**. Pull the cap out with a fingernail in the notch, then plug the Dupont 1x6. Pin 1 (GND) is at the "1" mark, at the antenna end.
- **BOOT**: a flexure key in the lid. The tongue is 5 mm wide, 0.9 mm thick and hinged toward the front; the stem sits 10.5 mm from the hinge.
  - Gap from the stem to the switch is 0.6 mm nominal. Over the whole tolerance stack it stays between 0.14 and 1.06 mm, so **the lid never holds BOOT down**.
  - A full press at the largest gap is a 1.46 mm stroke and strains the tongue 1.79 %. At nominal the stroke is 1.0 mm, 1.22 %.
  - Press it 6 times for the Wi-Fi setup hotspot.
- **RESET**: a 2.2 mm pin hole with a guide tube, 1.3 mm over the switch (0.84 mm worst case). Use a paper clip.
- **LEDs**: light holes with light tubes, PWR (D4, red) and LINK (D3, green). Set `led_skin=0.4` for a thin diffusing window instead of an open hole.
- **BME280 corner**:
  - An L-shaped baffle hangs from the lid along the two milled slots. It sits 0.8 mm above the board and 0.65 mm from the breakout.
  - Beside the H3 head it is relieved, leaving 0.75 mm of wall.
  - A matching rib on the floor runs under the slots.
  - Vents: 80 mm² in the lid, 219 mm² in the right and front walls.
- **General vents**: 6 lid slots over the buck regulator and LDO, 7 slots in the left wall and 5 in the antenna bump. Set `vents_lid_general` / `vents_lid_bme` to `False` if the mounting spot can get drips or dust from above.
- **J3 knock-out**: the front wall has a window like J1's, closed by a 0.6 mm skin. Cut it out with a flush cutter if a J3 cable is ever fitted.
- **Mounting**, any of these:
  - 2 diagonal ears. Each has a 4.3 mm hole for an M4 / #8 screw and 2 zip-tie slots, 2.0 x 5.2 mm.
  - The **flat back**, with foam tape.

  The ear screws are 17.6 mm from the antenna.
- **Text**: "GREE BRIDGE" is raised letters in a 0.8 mm pocket, with the letter tops flush with the lid top, so it prints face down with no supports. The small legends are debossed 0.6 mm.

### Assembly order

1. Trim the U5 header pins on the back. Untrimmed (4.4 mm) still clears the floor by 0.6 mm.
2. Put the board in the base with the antenna toward the bump. Drive **3 x M3 x 6** through H1-H3 into the bosses, snug only: they are self-tapping in PETG.
3. Plug J1, lay the wires flat in the left window, and zip-tie the cable to the block under the window.
4. **Clip the lid on**: line up the lip, then press straight down until all 3 latches click.
5. Snap in the J5 cap and mount the case: ear screws, zip ties, or tape.

## Print

PETG, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 4 perimeters, 20 % gyroid infill, **no supports**, no brim needed. The base has a 0.6 mm chamfer on its bottom edge and the lid a 0.8 mm chamfer on its top edge, to absorb elephant's foot.

| Part | Orientation | Size on the bed | Material |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | floor down, as exported | 114 x 69.6 x 23.3 mm | 23.5 cm³ solid, about 25 g |
| Lid | **top face down** (the latch arms point up) | 80 x 69.5 x 15.9 mm | 10.9 cm³, about 12 g |
| J5 cap | flange down | 6 x 19.2 x 3.6 mm | under 0.3 g |

Overhangs:
- The latch catch faces are 45° overhangs in the lid's print orientation. The lead-in ramps face up.
- Each window ceiling in the base is a 7.8 mm bridge.
- The widest unsupported ledge is 3.6 mm (tie-tunnel top) on the base and 3.2 mm (BOOT dot) on the lid.

Hardware:
- **3 x M3 x 6 pan head screws** (ISO 7045 / ISO 14583, head 5.6 x 2.4 mm). Optionally add 3 x M3 heat-set inserts up to 4 mm long.
- 1 zip tie for J1, 2.5 mm (up to 3.6 mm).
- For mounting, either 2 x M4 / #8 screws, zip ties up to 4.8 mm, or foam tape.

## Fit check (summary of `out/fit_check.txt`)

Distances are exact mesh-to-envelope minimum gaps (manifold3d), measured on the OpenSCAD meshes in the assembled position. The latch preload is measured on the meshes: it is how far each hook must move inward to free it, and comes out at 0.298 mm on all 3.

| Item (height above board) | Gap to lid above | Smallest 3D gap to case |
|---|---|---|
| L1 inductor (4.5) | 10.20 | 5.45 |
| U4 ESP32 module (3.25) | 11.45 | 3.36 |
| SW1 RESET actuator (4.3) | 1.30 (guide tube) | 1.30 |
| SW2 BOOT actuator (4.3) | **0.60 (stem)** | 0.60 |
| SW2 body (3.5) | 1.40 | 1.40 |
| **J1 with XHP-5 plugged (9.8)** | **4.90** | 1.55 |
| J1 wires, 1.9 mm OD worst case (14.2) | 0.50 | 0.50 |
| J3 header / optional J3 plug (7.0 / 9.8) | 7.70 / 4.90 | 0.75 (baffle) |
| J5 pins (8.54) / Dupont 1x6, cap off | open | 6.47 / 1.03 |
| U5 BME280 breakout (6.0) | 5.70 | 0.65 (baffle) |
| M3 x 6 screw heads (2.4) | – | 1.05 to the lid, 1.27 to the nearest part |

- **Tolerance stack**. The lid sits on the walls and the board sits on the bosses, so these gaps move with the PCB thickness (±0.16, i.e. 1.6 mm ±10 %), the base boss height (±0.1), the lid feature length (±0.1) and the switch height (±0.1, assumed):

  | Gap | Nominal | Thick PCB (1.76) | Thin PCB (1.44) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | BOOT stem to switch | 0.60 | 0.14 | 1.06 |
  | RESET tube to switch | 1.30 | 0.84 | 1.76 |
  | Baffle above the board | 0.80 | 0.44 | – |
  | J1 wires to the lid | 0.50 | 0.24 | – |

- **Latches**: at most 1.84 % strain, preload 0.30 mm (0.10 worst case), engagement 0.50 mm (0.30 worst case).
  - The hooks and the base overlap only by the intended preload: no other contact between base and lid.
  - Each latch window is at least 4.3 mm from any other opening, vent, ear or the bump.
- **Walls**: the board is 0.50 mm from the wall on all sides. The lid lip is 0.30 mm from the wall. The cap is 0.30 mm from its recess and 0.15 mm from the opening.
- **Antenna**: there is 5.00 mm of air from the antenna end to the nearest plastic.
  - nearest screw: 33.6 mm
  - nearest boss or insert position: 32.0 mm
  - ear screws: 17.6 mm
- **Under the board** (5.0 mm gap): untrimmed U5 pins 0.6 mm, J1/J3 tails 1.5 mm. Bosses and rest ribs stay 1.1 mm or more from every THT pad. The mounting face is closed.
- **Mesh checks**: each part is one watertight body, and there is no base/lid/cap/board overlap apart from the latch preload. All 349 rows pass.

## Assumptions

- **Heights above the board** (datasheet maxima):
  - ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 3.25; L1 4.5
  - PTS645SL43: 4.3 to the actuator (±0.1 assumed), 3.5 body, 0.25 travel
  - XH header 7.0; XHP mated 9.8
  - J5: pins 8.54; Dupont housing 14.5
  - SMA 2.6, SOT-223 1.8, 1206 caps 1.8, LEDs 1.2
- **GY-BME280** sits flat on a 2.5 mm header spacer. Its top, including pins and solder, is taken as 6.0 mm above the board.
- **Below the board**: XH tails 3.4, J5 tails 1.7, U5 pins 4.4 if untrimmed, solder at the ESP32 centre hole 1.0.
- **J1 wires**: 5 wires at the crimp's maximum OD of 1.9 mm, inner bend radius 2.5 mm.
- **Print tolerance** ±0.1 mm on heights within one part. PCB 1.6 mm ±10 %.
- **Latch forces** use PETG E ≈ 2000 MPa and a friction coefficient of 0.3. They are estimates; the strain is the design limit.
- **Board screws**: ISO 7045 / ISO 14583 M3 pan head, 5.6 x 2.4 mm. A DIN 7985 head can be up to 6.0 mm, which leaves 0.85 mm to the lid at H3.

## Before you print

The case was designed and checked in CAD only; it has **not been printed yet**. Things to check for your own unit:

1. **Where it goes in the unit**: for example on the side of the electrical box or on the chassis next to it. Keep the antenna bump at least 15 mm from sheet metal, ideally pointing toward the front grille. If the spot can get drips or dust from above (under the coil or the drain pan), turn the lid vents off (`vents_lid_general`, `vents_lid_bme`).
2. **Cable exit**: the J1 cable leaves through the left wall at lid height. A top exit would make the case about 3.5 mm lower but is not drawn.
3. **Your board**: measure the PCB thickness. The BME280 breakout top must be 6.0 mm or less above the PCB, and the J1 wires 1.9 mm OD or less.
4. **LEDs**: open holes (default) or a thin diffuser skin (`led_skin=0.4`, natural or white PETG).

On the first print, check:

1. **Latch click and hold.** All 3 should click with a firm press and the lid should not rattle. If they are too tight, reduce `latch_preload` (0.3); if too loose, raise it or `latch_hook` (1.1). The release should work with a small screwdriver through each window.
2. **Hook catch faces** (45° overhang, printed top face down): check they are clean, with no droop on the outer edge.
3. **BOOT**: with the lid on and the board powered, Tasmota must not see BOOT pressed. Six presses must each register. If a press doesn't register, lower `stem_gap`, but keep the thick-PCB worst case above 0.
4. **RESET**: a paper clip should reset the board, and the lid alone should never do it.
5. **Screw bite**: the M3 x 6 should hold firmly in the 2.5 mm pilots. If a pilot strips, go to 2.4 mm or use heat-set inserts (`use_inserts=True`).

After changing a knob in `layout_gree.py`, run `./build.sh` again: it rebuilds the STL and STEP files, reruns the fit check (exits 1 if anything fails) and the renders.
